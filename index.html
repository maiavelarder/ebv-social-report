How the corrected numbers are calculated

Followers excluding Costa Rica = all followers × (100% − Costa Rica share).
Net new excluding Costa Rica = this month minus last month, both calculated the same way. This counts only followers gained outside Costa Rica, so growth is never inflated.
Growth excluding Costa Rica = net new excluding Costa Rica ÷ last month's followers excluding Costa Rica.
Total followers for past months are rebuilt from today's count minus each month's follows and unfollows, as reported by Meta Business Suite.
Engagement rate = interactions ÷ reach. It rises when reach falls, so read it together with reach.
Months marked EST use an estimated country split (today's split) because Meta only shows the current audience snapshot. Until real monthly snapshots build up, growth excluding Costa Rica equals overall growth for those months. Months marked PARTIAL are month to date.
